# Product Requirements Document (PRD) — CampusHub

## 1. Executive Summary

**CampusHub** (Unified AI Campus Platform) is an institutional Progressive Web Application (PWA) designed to replace fragmented, siloed campus systems (separate portals for fees, attendance, examinations, leave/OD approvals, complaints, hostels, transport, clubs, library, lost & found, and announcements). 

Rather than treating a campus like a generic SaaS dashboard, CampusHub models the physical reality of academic life: buildings, schedules, attendance thresholds, mentor-student relationships, maintenance work orders, transit lines, and verified campus knowledge.

---

## 2. Target Personas & Roles

| Role | Primary Objectives | Key Pain Points Solved |
|---|---|---|
| **Student (Hosteller / Day Scholar)** | Track attendance, view timetable & exam schedules, pay dues, apply for leave/OD, lodge tickets, track buses, consult AI assistant. | Eliminates switching between 5+ portals or paper slips; prevents sudden exam debarment via proactive attendance monitoring. |
| **Faculty Member** | Mark lecture attendance, view assigned subjects & teaching schedules, post academic announcements. | Replaces paper registers; ensures instant synchronization with student dashboards. |
| **Mentor / Proctor** | Review and approve/reject leave and On-Duty (OD) applications for assigned wards; monitor ward attendance. | Streamlines approval chains; provides contextual ward statistics before approving absence. |
| **Administrator** | Oversee institute operations: manage students, academic timetables, fee schedules, exam schedules, announcements, user roles, and audit trails. | Centralizes student lifecycle records and operational analytics in one portal. |
| **Maintenance Staff** | Review work orders assigned by department (electrical, plumbing, carpentry, civil, IT); update resolution status and remarks. | Transparent tracking of campus maintenance without lost paper tickets. |
| **Transport Staff / Driver** | Manage transit routes, bus allocations, student pickup points, and publish route coordinates/status. | Live communication of bus routes and schedules to enrolled commuters. |
| **Security Staff** | Verify gate passes generated by approved student leave/OD applications. | Eliminates forged paper gate slips. |
| **Club President** | Propose campus events, manage membership rosters, publish club bulletins. | Direct channel to reach students without unverified social media spam. |

## 3. Core Functional Modules

### 3.1 Authentication & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Unified login with email and password, protected by Argon2id cryptographic hashing.
- Role-gated route middleware verifying authorization server-side on every request.
- Immediate role-based dashboard landing redirection upon login.

### 3.2 Student Academics & Schedule
- **Attendance Tracker**: Visual percentage calculation with clear below-80% threshold warnings and eligibility status for examinations.
- **Timetable**: Day-by-day lecture slots, faculty details, and classroom numbers.
- **Exams & Results**: Published internal assessment and semester exam schedules, subject codes, grades, CGPA, and historical result cards.

### 3.3 Financial Services (Fees)
- Consolidated statement of tuition, hostel, transport, and exam fees.
- Breakdown of paid amounts, dues, deadlines, and simulated payment receipt generation.

### 3.4 Leave & On-Duty (OD) Workflow
- Form submission with reason, date range, destination, and emergency contact.
- Automated routing to the student's assigned faculty mentor.
- Mentor review panel with one-click approve/reject actions and mentor notes.
- Downloadable/displayable digital gate pass with QR code upon mentor approval.

### 3.5 Grievance & Maintenance Ticketing
- Structured complaint filing with categories (Hostel, Academic, Transport, Infrastructure, Mess, IT).
- Automatic generation of formatted ticket IDs (e.g. `CMP-2026-00124`).
- Status progression: `submitted` → `in_review` → `in_progress` → `resolved` → `closed`.
- Maintenance dashboard filtered by department assignments.

### 3.6 Hostel & Residential Module
- **Strictly Gated Visibility**: Students with `hostelStatus !== 'hosteller'` cannot see hostel tabs, menus, or data.
- Room allocations, block details, mess menu, warden contacts, and room maintenance requests.

### 3.7 Campus Transit & Commuting
- Route catalogs with stoppage points, timings, and designated driver details.
- Student bus pass status verification.
- Driver dashboard for route updates and status broadcasting.

### 3.8 Student Life: Clubs, Events & Notices
- Centralized club directory with membership request workflows.
- Upcoming campus events and workshops with registration capabilities.
- Categorized institutional announcements with target audience filters.

### 3.9 Campus Utilities
- **Central Library**: Catalog search by title, author, subject, or ISBN with availability indicators.
- **Previous Year Question Papers**: Searchable repository by subject code and examination semester.
- **Lost & Found**: Item claim board with photos, location found, and contact references.
- **Campus Navigation Directory**: Building, lab, administrative office, and amenity location guide.

### 3.10 Grounded AI Campus Assistant
- Natural language conversational agent for campus inquiries.
- Grounded strictly on the institute's knowledge base and the student's own verified data.
- Strict data scoping: student accounts cannot query unauthorized records of other students or faculty.
- Clear action guidance: assists users by providing instructions and direct links for official actions rather than performing irreversible operations autonomously.

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements

1. **Performance**: Page load under 2 seconds on 4G mobile connections; lightweight bundles under 700KB minified.
2. **Offline Resilience**: Progressive Web Application with service worker caching of the app shell and an institutional offline fallback screen.
3. **Security**: Zero plain-text credentials, HTTP-only secure cookies, strict CORS, request rate limiting, and audit logging of high-impact administrative actions.
4. **Institutional Aesthetics**: Clean, calm, high-legibility UI adhering to deliberate design tokens; avoids decorative AI-style clichés (overused gradients, neon glow, excessive cards).
